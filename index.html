MY BACKGROUND

From environmental research to geospatial data science.

My work connects environmental science, geospatial analysis, and predictive modeling. I started building my data science experience at Baylor University, where I worked on land-use research, environmental flows, and machine learning. I later expanded that work at NETL through Leidos, developing national-scale geospatial models, data pipelines, and GIS applications.

My earlier work with WWF and NASA DEVELOP established the environmental and remote-sensing foundation that I brought into data science. Over time, I moved from research questions and spatial datasets toward reproducible modeling, large-scale processing, and tools that help researchers interpret results.

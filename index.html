MY BACKGROUND

From environmental research to geospatial data science.

My work connects environmental science, geospatial analysis, predictive modeling, and software development. I began my professional work in remote sensing and environmental analytics with NASA, WWF, and USGS. At Baylor University, I moved into more extensive machine learning, environmental flows, and data engineering work. I later applied those skills at NETL through Leidos, developing national-scale models, data pipelines, and GIS applications.

My earlier work with NASA, WWF, and USGS established the environmental and remote-sensing foundation that I brought into subsequent data science projects. Over time, I moved from research questions and spatial datasets toward reproducible modeling, large-scale processing, and tools that help researchers interpret results.

Screening millions of well records with geospatial machine learning

Geospatial data scientist · Label reconciliation, feature engineering and modeling

I developed a geospatial machine-learning workflow to identify likely orphaned-well candidates within a national inventory of roughly 7–8 million records. Basin-specific XGBoost models, training-based threshold selection and SHAP analysis exposed regional differences and informed a separate Appalachian experiment using additional Enverus attributes.

  • Python
  • XGBoost

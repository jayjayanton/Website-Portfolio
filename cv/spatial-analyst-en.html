Jay Antonio Oliver

Spatial Analyst

Quito, Ecuadorjayjayoliver@hotmail.com+1 (540) 406-9434LinkedInPortfolio & case studies

Profile

Spatial analyst with 6+ years of experience turning satellite observations and geographic data into environmental maps, watershed analyses and decision-support tools. Combines ArcGIS, Python and R with research across NASA DEVELOP, USGS, WWF, Baylor University and Leidos/NETL. Creator and sole developer of PredioAI; bilingual in English and Spanish.

Core skills

GIS & mapping: ArcGIS Pro, QGIS, ArcGIS Enterprise, Experience Builder; raster/vector analysis, spatial joins and coordinate transformations.

Remote sensing: Landsat, Sentinel-2, MODIS, VIIRS, CALIOP; land-cover change, NDVI, satellite time series and watershed delineation.

Automation & analysis: Python, R, SQL, ArcPy, GeoPandas, Rasterio, GDAL, Shapely and pyproj; reproducible spatial processing.

Relevant experience

Leidos — Geospatial Data Scientist

Morgantown, WV · NETL research | Feb 2024 – May 2026

  • Converted more than 400 geologic rasters into standardized polygon layers and prepared basin-specific web maps for NETL atlas publication through ArcGIS Enterprise and Experience Builder.
  • Engineered spatial predictors from well locations, land cover and basin context for orphaned-well screening within a team-assembled national inventory exceeding 7 million records.

Baylor University — Lab Manager / Geospatial Data Scientist

Waco, TX | Apr 2023 – Feb 2024

  • Developed 30-meter U.S. land-use basemaps for five observation years spanning 2008–2019; combined land-cover, crop and irrigation data and compared fractional land-cover representations.

Baylor University — Geospatial Data Specialist

Waco, TX | Oct 2021 – Apr 2023

  • Automated watershed delineation from stream-gage coordinates using R and the Model My Watershed API; prepared boundaries and attributes supporting Texas environmental-flow and reference-stream analyses.

U.S. Geological Survey — Spatial Analyst

Remote | Jan 2021 – Jul 2021

  • Analyzed more than 20 years of Landsat imagery using tasseled-cap indices and transition matrices to characterize land-cover change.
  • Produced pixel-level change maps and analytical datasets with R, Python and ArcGIS Pro.

World Wildlife Fund — Data Scientist / Statistical Analyst

Remote / Washington, DC | Jan 2020 – Dec 2020

  • Integrated forest cover, terrain, infrastructure, protected areas and socioeconomic predictors; developed maps and logistic-regression analyses of deforestation patterns for conservation research.

NASA DEVELOP — Remote Sensing Data Scientist

Hampton, VA / Remote | Sep 2019 – Apr 2020

  • Processed more than 900 satellite/environmental datasets across two projects; prepared MODIS, VIIRS and CALIOP observations for Central America air-quality analysis and comparisons with San Salvador PM2.5.
  • Mapped vegetation and hydrologic conditions in Lambayeque, Peru, integrating NDVI, precipitation, evapotranspiration and soil-moisture observations to investigate mesquite forest decline.

Selected development

PredioAI — Creator & sole developer

  • Created PredioAI as sole developer, integrating Quito cadastral, zoning and valuation records through REST APIs into interactive parcel maps and comparison tools.
  • Implemented Shapely/pyproj spatial operations, footprint placement and reviewed IRM comparisons linked to user-calibrated PDF/CAD measurements.

Quito Urban Expansion — Independent research in progress

  • Prepared Sentinel-2 composites and mosaics for a Tumbaco pilot; developing the validation framework for planned urban segmentation and change analysis.

Professional Summary

Geospatial data scientist and developer with 6+ years of experience in environmental, energy and scientific research, spanning NASA DEVELOP, WWF, USGS, Baylor University and Leidos/NETL. Builds reproducible Python, R and SQL workflows, interpretable machine learning models and analytical products from satellite observations, regulatory records and large spatial datasets. Creator and sole developer of PredioAI, bringing geospatial analysis into a full-stack application through React, FastAPI, REST APIs and interactive PDF/CAD workflows. First author of research in Scientific Data and coauthor in River Research and Applications. Bilingual in English and Spanish, with experience translating research questions into data pipelines, maps, publications and usable web tools.

Research & Professional Experience

Leidos — Geospatial Data Scientist

Morgantown, WV | February 2024 – May 2026

Research supporting the U.S. Department of Energy’s National Energy Technology Laboratory (NETL).

  • Developed geospatial machine learning workflows to screen for likely orphaned wells within a team-assembled national inventory of more than 7 million records; reconciled state-specific orphan lists, well identifiers and inconsistent source attributes.
  • Engineered temporal, categorical and spatial predictors from well dates, type, depth, state, land cover and location, including missing-data indicators and nearest-known-orphan distances calculated from training-reference records.
  • Trained and tuned Random Forest and XGBoost classifiers, including basin-specific models, Bayesian hyperparameter search, stratified cross-validation and training-derived decision thresholds; evaluated precision, recall and PR-AUC and interpreted model behavior with SHAP.
  • Developed an Appalachian Basin enrichment study covering approximately 900,000 wells, integrating available Enverus attributes to investigate regional modeling improvements and differences in data coverage.
  • Built Python and SQL workflows for Texas Railroad Commission data, parsing fixed-width wellbore files, recovering and propagating identifiers, enriching Well Header records and connecting them with production datasets.
  • Automated acquisition and preparation of 25 years of Kentucky well records with Python, GeoPandas, Selenium and BeautifulSoup, reducing data-processing effort by approximately 80% for that workflow.
  • Automated extraction and standardization of EPA Greenhouse Gas Reporting Program data for 2010–2024, combining API-derived facility tables, FLIGHT classifications, browser extraction and pandas transformations into reusable facility-level outputs.
  • Developed multi-output XGBoost workflows for ten coal-ash oxide constituents, investigating separate-estimator and shared-tree approaches, compositional log-ratio transformations, grouped validation and constituent-level error diagnostics.
  • Automated conversion of more than 400 geologic rasters into vector polygon datasets; standardized and quality-checked layers and prepared basin-specific web maps for NETL atlas publication through ArcGIS Enterprise, Experience Builder and the Energy Data eXchange.

Jay Antonio Oliver

Geospatial Data Engineer

Quito, Ecuadorjayjayoliver@hotmail.com+1 (540) 406-9434LinkedInPortfolio & case studies

Profile

Geospatial data scientist and developer with 6+ years of experience turning satellite observations, regulatory records and large spatial datasets into reproducible analytical workflows. Builds Python and SQL pipelines across APIs, databases and legacy files, with attention to identifiers, coordinate systems and data quality. Creator and sole developer of PredioAI. Bilingual in English and Spanish, with research experience spanning NASA DEVELOP, WWF, USGS, Baylor University and Leidos/NETL.

Core skills

Data engineering: Python, SQL, R, pandas, NumPy, ETL, REST APIs, PostgreSQL, fixed-width files, Linux, Bash and Git.

Geospatial processing: GeoPandas, GDAL, Rasterio, ArcGIS Pro, ArcGIS Enterprise, Experience Builder, Shapely, pyproj; raster/vector processing, spatial joins, coordinate transformations and satellite time series.

Software and validation: FastAPI, React, Leaflet, PyMuPDF, ezdxf, GitHub Actions, automated checks, data standardization and technical documentation.

Relevant experience

Leidos — Geospatial Data Scientist

Morgantown, WV · NETL research | Feb 2024 – May 2026

  • Automated 25 years of Kentucky well records, reducing processing effort by approximately 80%; standardized EPA facility data for 2010–2024 through APIs, browser extraction and pandas.
  • Converted more than 400 geologic rasters to quality-checked vector layers and prepared basin-specific web maps for NETL atlas publication.

Waco, TX | Apr 2023 – Feb 2024

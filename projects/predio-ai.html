The problem & my contributionFrom idea to working product

PredioAI began with a practical question: how can a homeowner make better use of a plan when preparing a renovation, changing a layout or exploring an extension? Relevant information is often spread across drawings, cadastral records, municipal planning documents and separate maps.

I designed a workflow that connects those sources and lets people inspect geometry, review measurements and place a proposed building footprint on a parcel map. I am responsible for the product design, responsive interface, Python services, data integration, analytical workflows, testing and deployment.

The intended users include homeowners, remodelers and construction professionals. Parcel discovery and comparison complement the plan workspace, connecting everyday home improvements with broader property decisions.

What the application doesPlans, properties & decisions
  • Explore property data. Search and filter Quito parcels, inspect cadastral attributes and valuation data, compare selections and work with municipal map layers.
  • Work with architectural plans. Inspect PDF/CAD geometry, confirm units or calibrate a known length, select elements and calculate measurements with manual review.
  • Study a proposed layout or extension. Create scaled geometry, locate a footprint on the parcel map and compare it with reviewed municipal parameters, including available setback information.
  • Carry the analysis forward. Organize property selections and export supported measurements and spatial data in formats such as CSV, JSON and GeoJSON.

For business and geospatial analytics, this demonstrates the complete path from data acquisition to an interactive decision tool: integrating different sources, making comparisons understandable and producing outputs that can be reviewed or reused.

Measurements rely on confirmed scale and user review; planning comparisons rely on the property information and municipal parameters supplied. These outputs support project preparation and professional review.

Computer vision & continued developmentPlanned extensions

The current plan workflow reads native PDF/CAD geometry. Further development explores targeted computer vision for scanned or image-based plans that need visual interpretation, with user review of the extracted elements.

Related work in my portfolio includes OpenCV-based image extraction and Quito urban expansion research, where satellite data preparation supports planned computer vision segmentation experiments.

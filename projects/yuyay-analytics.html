The problem

What needed to be understood or built?

A multidisciplinary consultancy needs to explain its services without flattening distinct areas of expertise. Visitors should be able to identify relevant work, understand who contributed, and follow claims to research, evaluation reports or live examples.

My contribution

The work I was responsible for.

I designed the site structure, visual presentation and frontend interactions, organized research and evaluation content, integrated assets and supporting links, and deployed the website. I also added applied software examples that connect analytical capabilities to tangible tools while preserving clear attribution.

Approach

How I worked through the problem.

01

Build a coherent content structure

Organize Home, Services & Analytics, Projects, Countries, Publications and Contact around consistent navigation. Present the team near the end of the homepage, with supporting biography content on the About page.

02

Make experience explorable

Use JavaScript project cards, country and theme filters, and keyword search to let visitors browse relevant work. Distinguish evaluation assignments, research contributions and independent software instead of implying that every example represents the same kind of engagement.

03

Connect claims to evidence

Link project descriptions to public technical reports, NASA DEVELOP materials, evaluation documents, the 2025 Scientific Data publication and live software where appropriate. Include downloadable CVs and research references.

04

Create a consistent responsive presentation

Combine scenic photographic heroes, regional imagery, reusable colors and typography, mobile layouts and lightweight interactions. Bilingual homepage and showcase content supports English and Spanish visitors.

05

Deliver a lightweight public website

Version the static files with Git and publish through GitHub Pages. The inquiry form prepares an email in the visitor’s own email application, avoiding an implied backend email-delivery service.

Result & scope

What the work delivered.

A live consulting website makes the team’s services, project history, research and software examples publicly accessible. Visitors can browse projects, filter by topic or location, follow evidence links, download CVs and begin an inquiry through their email application.

Scope & next steps

  • The contact form prepares an email in the visitor’s email application; it is not a backend delivery or customer-management system.
  • Bilingual content is implemented in the homepage and applied-analytics showcase; this case study does not claim complete translation of every interior page.
  • The project demonstrates website design and frontend development. Its portfolio category is Web Development, even though the consultancy presents analytical services.

Learning & progression

How this work develops my practice.

Content architecture is a technical design problem as well as an editorial one. Clear attribution, predictable filters and consistent links make a broad consulting portfolio easier to navigate and easier to trust.

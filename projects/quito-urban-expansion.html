ONGOING RESEARCH · COMPUTER VISION · DATA EXTRACTION

Quito Urban Expansion

Monitoring changes in Quito's eastern valleys through multitemporal Sentinel-2 imagery, reproducible data extraction, and future computer vision models.

Project GitHub Repo
01Purpose & study areaWhy this matters

Urban development across Nayón, Cumbayá, Tumbaco and Puembo creates a practical need to understand where built-up land is expanding and how landscapes change over time. I am developing a reproducible remote-sensing workflow to examine those patterns and investigate applications for land-use planning and construction-related spatial assessments. The present imagery is a pilot near Tumbaco, not a validated map of all Quito.

Satellite monitoring may help flag broader development patterns, but Sentinel-2's 10 m imagery is not a substitute for cadastral surveys, architectural blueprints or government-approved construction documentation.

03MethodologyCurrent and planned

Research workflow

01 · AcquireSentinel-2 L2A scenes via Sentinel Hub or CDSE S3
02 · PreprocessCloud screening, temporal composites, aligned tiles and mosaics
03 · AnalyzeNDVI, NDBI and candidate built-up land masks
04 · ModelInvestigate PyTorch CNN segmentation and change detection
05 · ValidateIndependent reference samples, spatial/temporal holdouts and error analysis

Current repository work includes acquisition scripts, annual/quarterly/monthly benchmarking, a CDSE S3 fallback, spatial mosaicking, tests, and sample composites. Planned analysis will compare consistent grids and time windows, derive spectral indices, assess candidate labels such as ESA WorldCover built-up class, and test whether computer vision improves built-up mapping. Segmentation accuracy and urban expansion rates have not yet been established.

04Expected applicationsResearch value

The intended outputs include interpretable built-up land masks, spatial change summaries and maps showing where further field or high-resolution imagery checks may be warranted. This combines Python data engineering, raster GIS, remote sensing, model evaluation and reproducible research. It is an exploratory decision-support project, not a regulatory or permit-approval tool.

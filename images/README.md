# Portfolio images and evidence

This folder contains committed research figures, application screenshots, camera images, workflow illustrations and supporting visual assets. The six land-use figures and the other local assets listed below are already present; no separate upload step is required to display them.

The authoritative image references, alt text, captions and evidence classifications are in [content/case-studies/](../content/case-studies/). Update those canonical records and rebuild the site when changing how an image is presented. Preserve established filenames and image URLs.

## Illustrations and supporting assets

- `Appalachian Basin Well Classification Map.png` is a conceptual illustration with synthetic well locations and probabilities. It is not a prediction map or quantitative validation output.
- `Coal_Ash_Concept.png` is conceptual cover artwork, not a laboratory sample or model result.
- `EPA_ETL_Pipeline.png` and `Texas RRC Wellbore Integration Workflow.png` illustrate workflows; they are not measured performance results.
- Legacy cover art, SVG placeholders and social marks are supporting presentation assets. Their presence in this folder does not make them research evidence.

Keep illustration and screenshot labels visible in captions. Do not replace original scientific figures with generated recreations, remove uncertainty context or imply that a collaborative application was solely authored by one contributor. PredioAI uses its local screenshot and public application link, with no dependency on implementation-source access.

Comparing satellite aerosol observations with ground evidence

NASA DEVELOP team researcher · Data processing and regression analysis

I contributed Earth-observation automation and regression analysis to a NASA DEVELOP air-quality study in Central America. Partner-supplied PM2.5 records in El Salvador enabled detailed satellite-product comparisons, while a separate CALIOP–Aqua MODIS analysis examined agreement between lidar and passive measurements of atmospheric aerosols.

Geospatial analysisNASA DEVELOP research

Linking vegetation change with water availability in Peru

NASA DEVELOP team researcher · Data preparation and time-series analysis

I contributed satellite-data preparation and environmental time-series analysis to a NASA DEVELOP study of mesquite forest decline in Lambayeque, Peru. The team combined vegetation, rainfall, evapotranspiration and soil-moisture evidence to investigate changing conditions, while distinguishing observed associations from an established cause of forest mortality.
